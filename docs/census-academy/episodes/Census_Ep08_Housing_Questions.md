## Transcript

This episode focuses on housing measures. Census data becomes useful when the population, measure, geography, and time period are stated before anyone searches for a number.

Housing questions need both a unit and a universe. Total units, occupied units, owner-occupied units, renter-occupied units, households, and people in households support different measures.

ACS topics include tenure, vacancy, structure type, year built, costs, value, rent, rooms, bedrooms, vehicles, and housing-cost burden. Definitions and denominators determine whether two percentages can be compared.

Survey evidence describes conditions. It does not by itself establish zoning capacity, project feasibility, legal compliance, or the City's adopted housing program. Pair it with local planning records while keeping each source's role distinct.
