## Transcript

This episode focuses on geographic fit. Census data becomes useful when the population, measure, geography, and time period are stated before anyone searches for a number.

Census geography is a system of legal, administrative, and statistical areas. A place, county subdivision, tract, block group, public use microdata area, and ZIP Code Tabulation Area are not interchangeable.

Choose geography based on the decision and product availability. Tracts and block groups support small-area analysis, but estimates can be less precise. Boundaries can change, and a service district or neighborhood may not match a standard Census area.

Save geographic codes, not only names. Use official relationship files or a documented crosswalk when boundaries differ. If an analyst aggregates areas, record the method and avoid claiming exact alignment where only approximation exists.
