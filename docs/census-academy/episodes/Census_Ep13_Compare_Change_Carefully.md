## Transcript

This episode focuses on comparisons over time. Census data becomes useful when the population, measure, geography, and time period are stated before anyone searches for a number.

A change claim needs compatible products, periods, universes, variables, and boundaries. First confirm that table concepts and question wording remained comparable, then inspect technical notes and annotations.

Test whether the difference exceeds sampling uncertainty. For dollar measures, decide whether the question concerns nominal or inflation-adjusted change and document the price index and reference year.

Boundary changes can create apparent demographic change without movement by residents. Use official relationship files where possible. Avoid overlapping five-year estimates for trend claims when a cleaner comparison is available.
