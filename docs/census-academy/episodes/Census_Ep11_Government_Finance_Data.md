## Transcript

This episode focuses on public-sector statistics. Census data becomes useful when the population, measure, geography, and time period are stated before anyone searches for a number.

The Annual Survey of State and Local Government Finances provides nationwide statistics on revenue, expenditure, debt, and assets. The Census of Governments supplies a broader recurring census of public organizations and activities.

Census government-finance categories support comparison across jurisdictions, but they do not mirror every line in a city's audited statements. Functional classifications, fiscal years, government coverage, and revisions must be checked.
