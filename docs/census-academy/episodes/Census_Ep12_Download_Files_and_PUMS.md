## Transcript

This episode focuses on bulk and microdata access. Census data becomes useful when the population, measure, geography, and time period are stated before anyone searches for a number.

Summary files support bulk access to published aggregate estimates. They preserve table structure and geography but require careful use of lookup files, sequence layouts, annotations, and margins of error.

Public Use Microdata Sample files contain disclosure-protected person and housing-unit records for custom analysis. They are samples, require weights, and identify only selected large geographies. Public Use Microdata Areas are the most detailed geography identified.

Never infer that a PUMS record represents a named person or a specific neighborhood. Follow API and privacy terms, use replicate weights where required, and document recodes. A custom estimate needs the same uncertainty and universe checks as a published table.
