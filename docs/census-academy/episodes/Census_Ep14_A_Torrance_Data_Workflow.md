## Transcript

This episode focuses on end-to-end practice. Census data becomes useful when the population, measure, geography, and time period are stated before anyone searches for a number.

A defensible workflow begins with a written question, decision owner, population, measure, geography, period, and acceptable uncertainty. It then selects the Census program and the local primary source for their distinct roles.

Create a source register with titles, publishers, URLs, vintages, table or variable identifiers, universes, geographic codes, and retrieval dates. Save raw downloads and API requests before calculations.

Check margins of error, product compatibility, boundary changes, inflation, and disclosure constraints. Record every transformation and preserve a dated interpretation that distinguishes evidence from assumptions and adopted policy.

Torrance application segment. This local application is separate from the Census Academy instruction. The cited City of Torrance primary source is City of Torrance primary-source set, published at https://www.torranceca.gov/Government/About-Torrance. For a local analysis, assemble the About Torrance, General Plan, housing, transportation-analysis, and audit pages as separately labeled local records, then pair each claim with the appropriate federal dataset. Do not present City policy or administrative records as Census Bureau findings, and do not present a federal estimate as adopted City policy.

Keep a reproducibility packet containing the question, source URLs, exact vintage, table or query, geography codes, raw result, calculations, and uncertainty note.
